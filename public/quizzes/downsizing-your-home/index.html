A Simple Gut Check

Signs It Might Be Time

From our guide — these are the questions worth sitting with honestly.

Are you worried about stairs, bathrooms, or accessibility as you age?

Do you have multiple rooms you rarely — or never — use?

Are your housing costs squeezing your budget for travel, hobbies, or time with family?

Does your home cause more heartache than joy when you look around it?

Has home maintenance become physically or financially overwhelming?

Is your home no longer tied to your career, kids, or other obligations that once anchored you here?

If you answered yes to even two of these — it's worth a conversation.

Common Questions

Things People Ask Us

How do I know if I'm really ready to downsize?

Readiness is part practical, part emotional. On the practical side: unused rooms, rising housing costs above 30% of income, and maintenance becoming overwhelming are clear signals. Emotionally: if thinking about a fresh start lifts a weight rather than adding one, that's a significant sign. Our guide — and our free consultation — walk you through both dimensions.

What if my kids disagree about what's best for me?

This is one of the most common situations we navigate. Adult children care deeply, but they don't always agree — and sometimes the conversation becomes more about their comfort than yours. We can serve as a neutral, knowledgeable third party to help facilitate the family conversation with facts, options, and no pressure.

Should I consider a condo or a smaller house?

Both have real trade-offs. A smaller house gives you more privacy and space — but you still have a yard to maintain. A condo typically has lower maintenance, but HOA fees can offset the savings. The right choice depends on your lifestyle preferences, budget, and how much you value community vs. privacy. We help you think through both.

What will I do with all my belongings?

Start by thinking about what you want to keep — not what to get rid of. What do you actually use and love? That answer tells you how much space you really need. From there, we help you build a room-by-room plan and connect you to organizers, estate sale specialists, and donation resources who handle this transition every day.

How long does a typical downsizing process take?

It varies widely — anywhere from 2 months to over a year depending on how much preparation is needed, the current market, and how quickly you want to move. Starting early is almost always better than waiting until you're under pressure. A free consultation gives you a realistic timeline for your specific situation.

Will downsizing actually save me money?

In the long run, almost always — but there are real upfront costs to factor in (moving, staging, repairs). The bigger question for most people isn't whether it saves money — it's whether it frees up cash flow and equity that can fund the lifestyle they actually want in retirement. That's the conversation we help you have.
